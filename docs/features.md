# Features

- **Tables:** live public-table discovery and search, private tables, five-character room codes, and invitation links. Two to eight seats, configurable capacity, readiness, host controls, player removal in the lobby, and practice bots.
- **Rules:** everything in [rules.md](rules.md): configurable 0–12/13/14/15 decks, both 200-point victory modes, Draw/Bank, Prediction, Flip Three, Second Chance, frozen duplicates, stacking multipliers, combinations, 7–10-card bonuses, roulette, tied endings, rotating first seats, deck recycling, and the AI Hackathon reverse round with a dramatic reveal shown to every player.
- **Practice:** **Play a practice game** starts a four-seat game with three bots. During a solo bot game, the host can press **Trigger AI Hackathon** to reveal the card immediately; it is unavailable in games with multiple human players.
- **Game table:** every player's hand, clear effect prompts, score breakdowns, standings, round history, table chat, and rematches. **Full screen** expands the table and hides the main navigation (Escape exits). Hands show special cards above number cards; cards fade to a light gray shadow when a player banks or busts. Long rows scroll horizontally.
- **Accounts:** guest play plus optional username/password accounts on this server. Accounts preserve profile, appearance, and stats across devices. Round counts, best round, and best total update after every completed round; games and wins count once when a game finishes. Practice games count too.
- **Themes:** six complete UI themes, saved with your profile: **Neon Arcade** (default), **Velvet Club**, **Lucky Pop**, **Violet Glow**, the frontier-saloon **High Noon** (desert sunset, tumbleweeds, wanted-poster opponents, muzzle flashes, gunfight busts, and gold-coin wins), and the spooky-playful **Haunted Hollow** (a moonlit graveyard with flapping bats, a dangling spider, drifting fog, will-o'-wisps and a peeking ghost; tombstone opponents; a flickering jack-o'-lantern turn marker; bats, ghosts or candy from every button press; slime-and-thunder ghost busts; and trick-or-treat candy storms for big wins). Eight avatar colors and six card designs, including Prism Foil, Black Gold, and Aurora Glass.
- **Motion:** animated deals with a shine on new cards, score counters with point-change bursts, a seven-card bonus reveal, a safe-bank chip stamp, turn highlights, victory confetti, and a roulette wheel that lands on the server-confirmed ×0 or ×2 pocket. Animations can be skipped, and reduced-motion preferences show results immediately.
- **Audio:** optional sound effects and original synthesized background music (**Synthwave**, **Lounge**, **Disco**, **Western**, **Spooky**), chosen independently of the visual theme or following it. High Noon and Haunted Hollow also swap in themed sound effects: gunshots and spurs, or thunder, ghostly wails, cackles, owls and bubbling cauldrons. Settings are saved per device. Audio is synthesized locally with Web Audio, starts after interaction, and pauses in hidden tabs.
- **Resilience:** automatic reconnect, saved seats, durable games across restarts, disconnect handling, host transfer, version checks, command de-duplication, and server-owned randomness.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard-operable dialogs, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts for phones, and a built-in rulebook.
